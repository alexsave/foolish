KEY JOURNEY
F minor (home) -> walkdown F7 Eb Db Eb G7 (27-28) -> Ab major (relative, 29-56) -> walkdown to G7 (57-60) -> C minor (v, the only key where the hook is transposed, 61-88) -> Fm7 -> Ab7 (89-92) -> Db major (bVI, 93-122) -> C7#9 (123-124, V of home) -> F minor (125-156) -> loops to F minor. The hook's five notes F Ab C Bb Eb sit unchanged under three floors (Fm, Ab, Db); C minor is the foreign ground. Tempo: 96 -> 92 -> 88 -> 96 -> 90 -> 96.

THE QUOTE
One measure: the kick pattern of "Impeach the President" (The Honey Drippers, the most-chopped NY break) at Dusty: Loop bar 1 (global bar 29), KICK `x:1 r:0.5 x:0.5 r:1 x:0.5 r:0.5`, with the 808 on the same three hits. Bars 30 onward grow the suite's own boom-bap kick out of it (the and-of-2 kick becomes a dotted figure with a ghost pickup into beat 4).

DJ PARTS (how the moves are placed)
SCRATCH C4b / C4c / C4t, STUTTER, BRAKE and SPINBACK act on the sound that is already playing, so every bar that carries one keeps its parts written out in full; they are placed at phrase ends (bars 8 and 16 of each 16, bar 4 of each 8), at the one-bar kit rests (16, 80), in the two DJ sections (Needle drop, Cut) and the showcase (125-132). Backspins end Needle drop (4), Cut (24) and Needle (156); brakes end Hand-off (28) and Scratch (132); every other section boundary is a fill.
