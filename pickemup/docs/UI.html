The shape of this game belongs to a Mattel trademark that was enforced recently enough that a clone called UYES was pulled from Google Play by a DMCA takedown. What is not protectable is the game: this is the Crazy Eights family, which is public domain. What is protected is the name, the card art, the four-colour-only suits, the oval and the call-out word - so none of those are here. Suits are shape AND colour (circle teal, triangle amber, square violet, diamond slate), which keeps us clear of the trade dress and is also the only version a colourblind player can read. Pick ’Em Up · surface study · 26 September 2026

Shed seven cards without being caught holding one

No Swift - the drawing comes first. Every screen is at the size Messages gives: the expanded view per phone (screen less about 126pt of Messages chrome), 340pt collapsed on every phone, and the 300 × 195 bubble BubbleSnapshot bakes. The felt, card edge, fern back, wood pills, board insets, deck layering, seat ring, hand formula and every flight curve are foolish’s, lifted from the shipped Swift. The rules are RULES_AND_KERNEL.md; every word on this page is its section 6.

How it moves, before how it looks

Filled in first, per docs/MOTION_BEFORE_FLOW.md. One move has one animation per channel it can arrive through, and the cut between the action half and the settlement half is the kernel’s (PkEvent.half), never a list a host keeps.

ChannelWhenWhat moves
A stagedI draw, play, pass, say it or call someone, before Send The ACTION events as they happen: draws fly in and turn over, a card flies to the pile, the suit picker asks, the called fan rings. Nothing a settle event would show.
B at SendI tap the native arrow (didStartSending) The last turn’s SETTLE events and the seal’s catch verdict: skips, the direction TURN, penalty flights, Caught you! or Wrong call, WIN and the reveal. The catch verdict is the one event whose early display would leak (D5d).
C my replayI reopen my own bubble Both halves from the prior state, nothing masked for me.
D their bubbleI open a bubble of theirs Both halves of the newest bubble only, from the sender’s seat, their draws masked: a card flies out of their fan, because their hand is not in my picture.
E arrivalA bubble lands while I am open As D, from the bubble on screen to the new tip, plus my own hand re-dimming as playability changes. The only channel that changes what I may do while I am looking.
Undothe Undo pill, or x on the staged bubble A played card flies home, 500ms, no bulge. Drawn cards never fly back (D8): the draft has a floor at its last draw, and x rebuilds to the floor, not the parent (D9).
ConflictAnother bubble won the parent I staged against Real in this game in two ways: a turn race, and out-of-turn Last card! and Caught you! bubbles, which anyone can send at any time (4.8). My staged card flies home as foolish’s red retraction ghost, then the winning chain plays forward. Never a cut.

The one-card call-out, settled

Nothing announces one card, and no seat ever shows a count (1.8, D22). A player who plays down to one must Send that play first; from then on they may say Last card! in any bubble, in or out of turn, and a LAST stamp appears under their badge. Anyone else may tap their fan to put Caught you! in the bubble they are composing.

The window is until the next completed turn, not the next bubble (D4): with one-bubble windows, a wrong catch or somebody else’s Last card! would silently close the window on a different player, which is a rule nobody could explain. It still needs no clock and cannot be lost to a slow phone, and it is usually exactly one bubble. A right catch costs 2, a wrong one costs the catcher 1, and the verdict only ever shows at Send.
