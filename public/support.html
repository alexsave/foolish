Support

Foolish is a Durak (дурак) card game, playable inside iMessage and, offline, against computer opponents.

How to play

In Messages, tap the App Store icon in the compose bar, find Foolish, and tap New game. See About for the rules.

Something not working?

Email us at alexvsaveliev@gmail.com with a description of what happened and, if you can, which device and iOS version you are on. We read every message.

Delete your account

This page deletes your account and all associated data immediately. You can also do this from Settings inside the app.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for what we do and don't collect. The iMessage app is a separate product with its own policy, linked from there: it collects nothing at all.
