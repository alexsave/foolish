Privacy Policy

Foolish Cards for iMessage (shown as "Foolish" in Messages)

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This app collects no data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party code. Your moves travel as iMessages, the same way your texts do.

What this app is

Foolish Cards for iMessage is a two-player Durak card game played inside Apple's Messages app. It is a standalone iMessage app. There is no separate Home Screen app.

Data we collect

None. The app has:

  • no accounts and no sign-in,
  • no analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting,
  • no ads and no third-party SDKs,
  • no access to your contacts, location, photos, or anything else on your device beyond its own game data,
  • no in-app purchases.

The app makes no network connections of its own, and we do not operate any server for it. Nothing on our end can receive your data, so none of it is collected, stored, shared, or sold.

How your game moves travel

Each move is encoded into the iMessage bubble the app sends. That message contains the cards played and the nickname you type into the game, if you choose to enter one. It is delivered by Apple's iMessage service like an ordinary text and is visible only to the people in your conversation. Apple's handling of iMessage is described in Apple's privacy policy.

The app never sees who your contacts are. Messages identifies conversation participants to the app only by anonymous identifiers. The names shown in the game are nicknames players type in themselves.

What stays on your device

The app keeps a small local record on your device: which games you are in, which seat is yours, the latest state of each game, and the nickname you last typed, so you do not have to retype it. This is used to show your list of games in the Messages drawer. None of it leaves your device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes, we will update this page and the effective date.
