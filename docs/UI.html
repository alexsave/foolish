The sky is the progress bar
Both orbs rise on the right and set on the left, and where they sit is how far through the phase the village is - derived from how many living players still owe a move. At 0 of 19 the moon is only just up; at 19 of 19 it is going down. You can tell from a thumbnail, without reading a word and without counting bubbles, how close the night is to breaking.
It is the one piece of state everybody wants constantly and nobody wants to ask for, and it costs nothing: the count already exists, so the orb is the same number said in a way you do not have to read. The moon is always full. Werewolves.
One line of caption, because that is all Messages gives you. The caption under a bubble is a single truncating line, so everything else has to be inside the image - which is why the count sits in the bottom right of the art rather than under it.
The lobby has no orb position, because nothing is counting down yet - it gets the dusk sky and a moon that has only just risen, and the count is seats rather than moves.
The two endings are the same picture inverted. Wolves take a blood moon with the red glow around it; the village takes a bright day over the rooftops. They are the only two bubbles anybody screenshots, so they are worth the extra sky.
Nineteen near-identical night cards is the disguise working, and also a lot of thread. The count and the moon's position are the only things that change between them, which is what stops them being interchangeable.
"Ivo has slept" and "Dara has slept" are the same card. Your own move gets nothing beyond sitting on the right, or a scroll would show which bubbles were yours to anyone reading over your shoulder.
