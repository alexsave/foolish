Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe for iMessage (shown as "Ultimate" in Messages)

How to play

Open a conversation in Messages, tap the apps button next to the text field, and choose Ultimate. Send the invitation. Your friend taps it and plays first, as X. Take turns; each move is a new message. The rules are behind the book button on the board.

The app has no Home Screen icon: it lives in Messages, in the apps row next to the text field.
