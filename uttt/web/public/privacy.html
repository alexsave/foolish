Privacy Policy

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe for iMessage (shown as "Ultimate" in Messages), and the website uttt.live

Effective date: September 25, 2026

The app collects no data. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party code. Your moves travel as iMessages, the same way your texts do.

What this app is

Ultimate Tic-Tac-Toe is a two-player game played inside Apple's Messages app. It is an iMessage app only: there is no separate Home Screen app.

Data we collect

None. The app has:

  • no accounts and no sign-in,
  • no names or nicknames of any kind,
  • no analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting of its own,
  • no ads and no third-party SDKs,
  • no access to your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, or anything else on your device beyond its own game data,
  • no in-app purchases.

The app makes no network connections of its own, and we do not operate any server for it. Nothing on our end can receive your data from the app, so none of it is collected, stored, shared, or sold.

How your moves travel

Each move is encoded into the iMessage the app sends. The message holds the moves played so far, the time the game was started (used to draw the board), and, for each of the two players, a short code made from the anonymous identifier described below, so each device can tell which side is its own. That code cannot be turned back into the identifier. The message is delivered by Apple's iMessage service like an ordinary text and is visible only to the people in your conversation. Apple's handling of iMessage is described in Apple's privacy policy.

The app never sees who your contacts are. Messages identifies the people in a conversation to the app only by anonymous identifiers, which change if the app is reinstalled. The app uses them only to work out which side, X or O, is yours, and they are never sent anywhere except in that short code.

What stays on your device

The app keeps one small record on your device: which side is yours in your most recent games (up to 256 of them), so you stay the same player if Messages hands the app a new identifier. It never leaves your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Replay links and uttt.live

When a game is over, "Copy code" puts a link to it on your clipboard. The app does not send it anywhere; you choose whether to share it. The link contains only the moves of the game and the time it was started, which is used to draw the board. It says nothing about who played.

Opening a link on uttt.live plays the game back entirely in your browser. The site has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no ads, and it does not store the games it shows. Like any website, it is served by a hosting provider (Vercel), which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to operate and protect its service. We do not use those logs to identify or track anyone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app or the site changes what it does with data, we will update this page and its effective date.
