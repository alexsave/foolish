# The seed is set by poking sys_time (the one entropy source) just before the
# first press, so the deal does not depend on how long the ROM takes to start.
# Every command at frame N acts after frame N ran (gbrun), so an expect and a
# press on the same frame see the press: steps leave a frame between them.
#
# The selection: A toggles the cursor card (raised), A held with LEFT/RIGHT
# sweep-selects (toggling each card the cursor lands on), B clears, and UP
# plays the selected set. My attack; hand (diamonds trump): 6s As 6h Qh Kc 6d.
@99  poke _sys_time 1f 00
@100 press A
# the deal lands (README "The deal") before the first move
+1   until _fly_n 01
+0   until _fly_n 00
+1   press RIGHT
+6   expect _cur 00
# A raises the six of spades; B clears it again
+1   press A
+6   expect _selm 01 00 00 00 00
+0   shot shots/50_selected_one.png 3
+1   press B
+6   expect _selm 00 00 00 00 00
# left: the cursor wraps to the six of diamonds; A selects it
+1   press LEFT
+6   press A
+6   expect _selm 00 00 00 08 00
# right: back to the six of spades. Hold A (it selects the six of spades) and
# sweep right twice (the ace, the six of hearts), then left once (the ace
# again, deselected): the three sixes are selected, the cursor on the ace
+1   press RIGHT
+6   press A 40
+6   press A+RIGHT 2
+6   press A+RIGHT 2
+6   press A+LEFT 2
+6   expect _cur 01
+0   expect _selm 01 02 00 08 00
+0   shot shots/51_sweep_selected.png 3
# UP plays the selected three sixes at once
+20  press UP
+6   expect _G+74 03
+0   expect _selm 00 00 00 00 00
+0   shot shots/52_multi_attack.png 3
# Miami beats two and takes all three (the foot rail says so for a moment)
+90  expect _G+74 00
# the refill from the stock lands (README "The deal")
+0   until _fly_n 00
+4   shot shots/55_bot_takes.png 3
