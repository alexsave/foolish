# think_time.txt's game, with keys pressed while Moscow thinks: RIGHT then
# LEFT, A then A (a cursor move each way, a selection made and unmade, each
# drawn), which leave the hand as it was, so it stays the same game and the
# time a think takes with keys pressed compares decision for decision with
# think_time.txt (README "The screen while a bot thinks"). The presses that
# play still come only in the 20 frames after a move. The seed is poked into
# sys_time.
@99  poke _sys_time 07 00
@100 press DOWN
+8   press DOWN
+8   press DOWN
+8   press A
+1   until _screen 01
# the first think has no cursor yet; then 24 rounds with keys mid-think
+0   until _bot_thinking 01
+0   until _bot_thinking 00
+1   press RIGHT
+5   press UP
+5   press UP
+5   press DOWN
repeat 24
+0   until _bot_thinking 01
+4   press RIGHT
+6   press LEFT
+6   press A
+6   press A
+0   until _bot_thinking 00
+1   press RIGHT
+5   press UP
+5   press UP
+5   press DOWN
endrepeat
+0   peek _bot_lines_max 4
+0   peek _bot_lines_sum 4
+0   peek _bot_moves 2
+0   peek _moscow_playouts_sum 4
+0   peek _moscow_plies_sum 4
+0   peek _moscow_thinks 2
+0   peek _G+79 1
# the same game in any ROM: these do not depend on how long a think takes
+0   expect _bot_moves 19 00
+0   expect _moscow_playouts_sum 7a 03 00 00
+0   expect _moscow_plies_sum 24 81 00 00
+0   expect _G+79 02
