# The seed is set by poking sys_time (the one entropy source) just before the
# first press, so the deal does not depend on how long the ROM takes to start.
# Every command at frame N acts after frame N ran (gbrun), so an expect and a
# press on the same frame see the press: steps leave a frame between them.
# The bot acts again 30 frames after the board last changed, so the steps of
# one bout fit inside that window.
#
# TARGET mode, cover or pass: Miami leads the queen of spades; diamonds are
# trumps, and my queen of diamonds can beat it (a trump) or pass it on (a
# queen). Hand: 6h 9h Ah 8c Jc Qd.
@99  poke _sys_time 78 00
@100 press A
+1   until _G+74 01
+1   press RIGHT
+4   press LEFT
+4   expect _cur 05
+1   press UP
+4   expect _in_target 01
+0   expect _tgt_n 02
+0   shot shots/20_cover_or_pass_cover.png 3
# one step right of the last attack is the pass space
+1   press RIGHT
+4   expect _tgt_i 01
+0   shot shots/21_cover_or_pass_pass.png 3
+1   press UP
+5   expect _in_target 00
# passed: two queens on the table and Miami defends
+0   expect _G+74 02
+0   expect _G+76 01
+0   shot shots/22_passed.png 3
