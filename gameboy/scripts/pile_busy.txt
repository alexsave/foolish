# The seed is set by poking sys_time (the one entropy source) just before the
# first press, so the deal does not depend on how long the ROM takes to start.
# Every command at frame N acts after frame N ran (gbrun), so an expect and a
# press on the same frame see the press: steps leave a frame between them.
#
# The bot's hand and the pile share the top rows: the backs keep to columns 0-16
# (pixels 0..135) however many cards the bot holds, and the pile (columns 17-19)
# stays whole beside them. I give the bot 32 cards and the pile a card, then
# attack so the board is redrawn: 16 backs are shown (the rail has the count),
# the last ends at pixel 135 (its dark edge), the wool 136..138 beside the pile is
# clear, and the pile's black is at (141, 20) and (156, 20).
@99  poke _sys_time 1f 00
@100 press A
+19  poke _G+5 ff ff ff ff 00
+0   poke _G+80 02
+1   press RIGHT
+6   press UP
+10  expect _G+74 01
+0   pixel 135 20 2
+0   pixel 137 20 1
+0   pixel 141 20 3
+0   pixel 156 20 3
+0   shot shots/pile_busy.png 3
