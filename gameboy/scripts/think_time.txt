# Moscow's think time on decisions that do not depend on the ROM's timing: every
# press comes in the 20 frames after a move (Moscow waits 30 frames after the
# board last changed), never during a think, so a ROM that thinks slower
# plays the same game and the times compare decision for decision (README
# "The screen while a bot thinks"). The seed is poked into sys_time.
@99  poke _sys_time 07 00
@100 press DOWN
+8   press DOWN
+8   press DOWN
+8   press A
+1   until _screen 01
# 25 rounds: the game ends with the 25th move
repeat 25
+0   until _bot_thinking 01
+0   until _bot_thinking 00
+1   press RIGHT
+5   press UP
+5   press UP
+5   press DOWN
endrepeat
+0   peek _bot_lines_max 4
+0   peek _bot_lines_sum 4
+0   peek _bot_moves 2
+0   peek _moscow_playouts_sum 4
+0   peek _moscow_plies_sum 4
+0   peek _moscow_thinks 2
+0   peek _G+79 1
# the same game in any ROM: these do not depend on how long a think takes
+0   expect _bot_moves 19 00
+0   expect _moscow_playouts_sum 7a 03 00 00
+0   expect _moscow_plies_sum 24 81 00 00
+0   expect _G+79 02
