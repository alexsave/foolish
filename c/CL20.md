# CL-20 — octogen's successor

CL-20 (HNIW, the rung above octogen's HMX on the explosives ladder) is
octogen's engine forked verbatim plus the levers below. Registered as
`cl20`/`cl` (C-only arena/research bot, like torpex and novichok;
`cl20_oracle`/`clo` is the 6x-worlds audit variant). With every lever off it
is decision-identical to octogen (paired 0/0/N at every player count), so
octogen's validated dominance matrix (OCTOGEN.md, SEMTEX.md) transfers, plus
whatever each lever adds. Same legitimacy contract: public information only,
no LLM, nothing computed outside one `chooseMove` call.

All numbers are paired same-deal deltas vs an **octogen** control
(`cnitro_eval --control=octogen`): Δ = hero mean finish − control mean
finish, negative = cl20 better, `±` = SE of the paired delta, `b/w/e` =
deals cl20 finished better / worse / equal. Seeds are fresh per experiment.

## Where octogen's headroom is (the compute probe)

Before touching the bot, the 6x-worlds oracle (`octogen_oracle`) was paired
against octogen to see where more search still pays (200 pairs/cell, seeds
610001):

| tables | pc2 | pc3 | pc4 | pc6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cordite | −0.025 ± 0.043 | −0.100 ± 0.069 | +0.060 ± 0.090 | **−0.280 ± 0.109** |
| handwritten | — | −0.030 ± 0.049 | −0.075 ± 0.064 | +0.000 ± 0.105 |
| random | — | — | −0.015 ± 0.042 | +0.000 ± 0.081 |

Heads-up and 3–4 players are search-saturated (as SEMTEX.md found); the
6-player MC mirror is still **compute-limited**. 2x worlds at pc6 recovers
about half of it (−0.130 ± 0.117), wider candidate survival alone none
(+0.035 ± 0.109) — the binding resource is worlds, not breadth.

## Measurement infrastructure added

- `cnitro_elo` now schedules every game up front and plays them in parallel
  (`make OMP=1`), then applies the sequential K=32 Elo in schedule order
  (bit-identical trajectory to the old serial loop), and finally fits an
  order-free **Bradley-Terry maximum-likelihood rating** over the same
  pairwise seat outcomes with a game-level bootstrap (`--bootstrap=N`,
  default 400): per-bot 95% intervals, the top-two gap with its interval and
  P(gap > 0), and the direct head-to-head records. SEMTEX.md showed the
  sequential arena moves ratings by >100 points on game order alone; the MLE
  is the instrument that can call a ladder position definitive.

## The nulls (measured, so nobody retreads them)

Every lever below is implemented, sound, and default-off. Cells are 200-300
pairs unless noted; Δ vs the octogen control.

1. **Policy-consistency belief constraints** (`CL_HWC`). Every decision by a
   handwritten-family seat is a deterministic function of its hand, so each
   observed move is an exact constraint on that hand: a trump first-attack
   means the whole hand is trump; a cover with Q♠ over 7♠ means no 8♠–J♠; a
   GOOD with defender capacity left means no non-trump card of any table
   value; a throw-in short of capacity means those were its only matching
   cards; a pickup facing several attacks means its greedy full cover fails.
   Constraints persist across draws as count constraints ("at most d of
   these, d = cards drawn since") and drop on any contradiction (which also
   marks the seat non-handwritten). An oracle audit (`CL_VERIFY`, reads the
   true hands only to check) found 0 violations in 3,909 constraints against
   handwritten seats. Effect: null at every player count — handwritten
   tables pc2 −0.007 ± 0.017, pc3 −0.023 ± 0.040, pc4 −0.017 ± 0.047, pc6
   +0.067 ± 0.075, pc7 −0.013 ± 0.135, pc8 −0.093 ± 0.110; cordite tables
   pc7 +0.213 ± 0.150, pc8 −0.053 ± 0.153 (pooled ≈ −0.01 ± 0.015). This
   agrees with NOVICHOK.md: even the *complete* hand cheat is worth only
   ~0.09 at pc3 handwritten, so partial hand information has a low ceiling.
2. **Adaptive final duel** (`CL_ADAPT_K`): extend the top-2 stage while the
   paired same-world difference is inside 2 SE. Null everywhere (cordite
   pc2 −0.010, pc3 +0.020, pc4 −0.060 ± 0.057, pc6 −0.010; handwritten
   pc3/4/6 within ±0.05). Extending from stage 1 at pc6: −0.145 ± 0.122.
3. **Race deliberation** (`CL_RACE`): statistical pruning of candidates on
   paired deficits, world cap 3x. cordite pc6 −0.055 ± 0.135, pc2
   −0.065 ± 0.043; handwritten pc4 **+0.095 ± 0.062**. Unreliable; off.
4. **Self-search rollouts** (`CL_SELF`): my first post-root decision inside
   the playout chosen by search over my legal moves. cordite pc2/3/4 null;
   handwritten pc4 **+0.170 ± 0.072** — the in-world best reply exploits
   sampled hidden cards it could not know (strategy fusion), the same
   failure as octogen's opponent reply tournament.
5. **Self-seat MC-defender model** (`CL_SELFPOL=2`): cordite pc4
   +0.150 ± 0.087. Off.
6. **Espresso rollouts at heads-up only** (`CL_ROLLOUT=1`, struct path):
   cordite pc2 +0.060 ± 0.046. Off.
7. **Soft voids for strategic seats** (`CL_MCVOID`): keep an MC seat's
   voids as "no cheap same-suit cover" instead of dropping them. cordite pc3
   +0.070 ± 0.038. Off.
8. **Wider candidate survival at pc6** (`CL_KEEP1=6 CL_KEEP2=3`):
   +0.035 ± 0.109. Worlds, not breadth, are the pc6 resource.
9. **Live-deck exact evaluation** (`CL_XDECK`): heads-up with ≤ 2–4 deck
   cards, value every (world × candidate) by an exact sign-only solve of the
   rest of the game in that world (the solver now supports a fixed deck
   order: deterministic draws, full child clones, deck_n + per-world salt in
   the transposition key). Infeasible: at the 14–16 cards a heads-up
   pre-endgame holds, >90% of solves abort at 100k nodes (depth and budget)
   and a decision costs ~10x; even deck-empty 10-card leaf solves at 40k
   nodes are ~8x slower per game. Kept as research plumbing.
10. **Violation-count profiling** (`CL_PROFILE=2`): counting proven
    handwritten-consistency violations as weak-seat evidence flags cordite
    seats 48% and robusta 43% of the time vs random 29% — MC bots deviate
    from handwritten *more* than random does. Off.
